For Kevin · Why a website?

Rent the pitch,
or own the stadium.

Instagram, YouTube and Telegram built the audience. But you're playing on someone else's ground: one algorithm change and reach drops overnight. A website is the stadium you own, where fans, brands and opportunities come to you.

01

Own your audience

Email list, Telegram and site members belong to you. No algorithm can switch them off.

02

Sponsors that pay properly

Permanent placements, a media kit and click reports make you easy to buy, at a better rate than one-off story mentions.

03

Tours that sell out

Announce trips weeks ahead, collect RSVPs and sell local sponsorship for each city before you land.

04

Found on Google

Search "Ultimate Kombo" or "El Clásico prediction" and land on you: your analysis, your contact, your brand.

05

Credibility for access

Clubs, players and press take a polished home seriously. More tunnel moments, more interviews.

06

One home for everything

Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, tour dates and brand enquiries, one link that does it all.
